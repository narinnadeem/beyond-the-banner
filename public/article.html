Two local laptop repair shops, compared

Review by the Campus Tech Desk

Byte Bay

Byte Bay is the cheaper option. Screen replacements start at a flat fee and most repairs are done in two days. The staff explain the problem in plain language and send photos of the work. The downside is a small waiting area and no weekend hours, so drop-offs must happen on weekdays.

Warranty on parts is 90 days. Data is not backed up unless you ask, which adds a small charge.

FixForward

FixForward costs about a fifth more, but includes a free data backup with every repair and offers Saturday appointments. Turnaround is slower, usually four to five days, because parts are ordered after the diagnosis.

Warranty on parts is 180 days, and the shop will re-check the laptop for free within a month.

The verdict

If speed and price matter most, Byte Bay is hard to beat. If your laptop holds work you cannot lose, or you can only visit at weekends, FixForward is the safer choice.
